The Lines Between

For as long as there have been those forces which distinguish between that which had form, and that which lacked it, there have been lines which divided them. When the Material Realm coalesced from the primordial thought, the lines which marked its ends became the first form of what you might conceive to call ‘the Aegis’, though no proper name for it yet existed. The boundaries of this land, much like any other among the Formless, were, and still are to this day, variable and malleable. As such, the various barriers have waxed and waned over the millennia, affected by both natural forces and conscious interventions from interested parties.

The Aegis, in the form you best understand it, was formed during the Material Plane’s Sundering, dividing the one world into two skies and scattering the other realms further beyond reach, for each world was to be estranged from the Formless, the Otherside, the Geestryk. This estrangement too had its variability world to world, but it did hold for many centuries.

But what was Broken must be made Whole. The convergence, the Syzygy, was fated to come to pass and this great and terrible alignment has warped all boundaries and divisions, leaving them open to influence, either to strengthen or weaken to any actor's taste.

As for the Host of Hemel, the matter is deceptively simple. They subject themselves to the laws, both supranatural and natural concerning the coming and going between realms, and while petty spirits who dwell within and around the Bounds of the Material have little effect upon it when passing through those lines, the spirits of distant Hemel and its Throne are liable to shatter the barriers and bounds if they were to pass into the Material, for such is the price paid for such depth and purity of existence.
