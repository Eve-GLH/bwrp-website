Skills

BWRP uses a mixture of plugins to present a suite of useful skills to help give each character their own unique flavour, and provide some helpful tools for their players. These skills can be broken into three categories: Player, Basic and, Advanced skills.

Player skills include basic features that make day-to-day life a little easier on the server, such as being able to Carry other players.

Advanced skills are a pool of non-standard skills able to be distributed by Storytellers to help provide mechanical backing to certain narrative paths.

The most frequently used group of skills are the Basic skills. Each character is given 20 Skill Points to buy from a large pool of skills to master certain weapons or craft, among other things. Many Basic skills have a variety of cost, as well as other Skill or Race prerequisites.

Our skills are frequently updated and we are always adding newly requested skills for characters to buy. For the most accurate and up to date information on the skills themselves, please check their tooltips in game.

Once you have passed through spawn enough to be prompted you can view the skills menu with the command /Class Skill. Here you may open any category of skills to explore their functions through their tooltips. To purchase a skill in this menu simply left click it.

Skills may then be directly cast by using /Class Cast [SkillName] or may be bound to cast when clicking with an item with /Class Bind [SkillName].

Skills may be reset by passing back through spawn. For characters already in play, they will need the help of a Storyteller to facilliate this.

Player Skills

  • Basic Book Binding Cast this while holding a book to apply a custom skin to the book. Works on written or unwritten books.
  • Carry Cast this while looking at a player to pick them up.
  • Empty Belly Cast this to empty your hunger bar a little so you can eat some more food!
  • Rename Cast this to gain a little XP and open an anvil GUI for easy item renaming.
  • Walk Cast this skill to toggle between a slow walking speed and your usual speed.

Racial Skills

Whilst some skills within the pool of Basic skills have requirement of Race, many are optional choices. The skills documented below are meant to represent the simple biological facts of life for their races.

Myriad

Patch Job Myriad can use an iron block to make quick patches to their metal plates. They can cast this to gain 20 absorption HP and a full heal after a short channel.

Iron Body When Myriad fall from a high place, they have majorly reduced fall damage. This ability can be cast to toggle on and off a small AoE of damage around their impact site.

Clang/Clong High/Low/Mid Myriad can make various metalic noises on command in a myriad of tones.

Pellet Clang Once every few days a Myriad with a full belly can cough up a Strange Pellet!
