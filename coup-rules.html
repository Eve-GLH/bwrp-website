Parravonian Coup

A very original game, entirely not stolen (Honest!) from the great game; Coup

In the not too distant past, the government is "run" for unknown reasons by a new “class” of people who came out of the mist. Their incompitence and poor control of the economy has reduced all but a privileged few to lives of poverty and desperation. Out of the oppressed masses rose nobody who mattered who focused on overthrowing these powerful rulers. The valiant efforts of pure luck have created discord, intrigue and weakness in the political tearooms of the washups, bringing the government to brink of collapse. But for you, a probably also incompitent official, this is your opportunity to manipulate, bribe and bluff your way into fleeting power. To be successful, you must destroy the influence of your rivals and drive them into exile. In these turbulent times there is only room for one to survive.

Setup

Number of players: 3-6

Object: To be the only player with any cards left.

Materials: Deck of cards, treasury of coins.

Setup: Shuffle the cards and deal two to each player. Players should look at their cards but keep them hidden from everyone else. Each player takes two coins from the treasury as their starting wealth.

Cards: There are five different characters in the deck, and three copies of each character. Each of the five characters has one or more special abilities.

Cards

Assassin:

Pay 3 coins to assassinate another player (that player discards one card).

Bureaucrat:

Take 3 coins from the treasury, then pick another player and give them a coin from your reserve.
Can block foreign aid.

Captain:

Steal 2 coins from another player.
Can block a Captain.

Contessa:

Can block an Assassin.

Inquisitor:

Take 1 card from the deck and 1 card from a target player. Return 1 card to the deck and 1 card to the target. (The target picks the card they give. When using an Inquisitor on a target player, you may switch your own cards with those from the deck or the target player.)
Can block a Captain.
Can block an Inquisitor.

Play

Starting with the player to the left of the dealer and going clockwise, players take turns performing one of the available actions.

  1. Income: Take one coin from the treasury. This cannot be challenged or blocked.
  2. Foreign Aid: Take two coins from the treasury. This cannot be challenged but it can be blocked by the Bureaucrat.
  3. Coup: Costs seven coins. Cause a player to give up a card. Cannot be challenged or blocked. If you start your turn with 10 or more coins, you must take this action.
  4. One of the actions listed on a character card. You may lie about which cards you have in hand, but be warned: another player may challenge you!

Blocking: If another player takes an action that can be blocked, any other player may block it by claiming to have the proper character on one of their cards. The acting player cannot perform the action and takes no other action this turn. The acting player may choose to challenge the blocking player. If they win the challenge, the action goes through as normal.

Challenge: When the acting player declares their action, any other player may challenge their right to take the action. They are saying "I don't believe you have the proper character to do that." The acting player must now prove that they have the power to make the action, or lose the challenge. If they have the right character, they reveal it and place the revealed card back in the deck. They then shuffle the deck and draw a new card. The challenging player has lost the challenge. If they do not have the proper character, they lose the challenge.

Losing a Challenge: Any player who loses a challenge must turn one of their cards face up for all to see. They can no longer use this card. If that is their last card, they are out of the game.

Losing: Any time a player loses a card, they choose which of their cards to reveal. When you have no more cards in hand, you lose the game.

Optional Rules: Teams

Setup:

There are teams starting with an even distribution. The first player chooses a team, and everyone alternates teams going clockwise from them. This means that - should there be an odd number of players - the first and last players would end up on the same team.

New Rules:

A player cannot Coup, Assassinate, Steal from nor block a Foreign Aid attempt by another player of the same team unless all remaining players are on the same team.

If there is only one team in play, you may target any other player. You may also change the team of someone else to make them the sole target of the rest of the players left in the game.

New Actions:

There are two new actions:

  • A player may pay 1 coin to change their own team.
  • A player may pay 2 coins to change the team of another player.
