The Calling of Prudence

The responsibility of summoning Prudence was a multi-faceted affair, stretching back to the days when Hemel’s conclave gathered ages ago. This heavenly council carefully set forth the who, what, when, and how of each Archon, plucking the tiniest strands of fate to set things in motion, showing to the mortal peoples a path they’d have the greatest chance to follow.

From this long road arose Mathias Audaire, the last to hold that baton. Fresh from casting off the dark cloak of the inquisition, his time was unique to itself, living under the weight of two great demiurges, playing out their own grand schemes with his fellow survivors as pawns in a game too grand to see. To many, the Archons were myths or legends, few would carry on the beliefs Audaire had when there were so many more forceful beings haunting Parravon. Mathias, however, held a deeper purpose, and would make every effort to bolster his God’s name. In doing so, he was able to build a grand temple, inspiring even those who had only learned of the Archons upon arriving at Nebelloren. The knowledge Mathias had was of legend and folklore alone, and he set about attempting every myth he could in hopes that a miracle could happen. His temple was founded on the old bridge, a place of two miracles already. He restored old traditions, cuisine, and festivities, attempting to relight the very soul of Variman in a strange new land.

Through these efforts, and the growing beliefs of those around him, the stage was set for Hemel to grant another Archon presence. The congregation on the night of Felsite 24, in the 13th year of Parravon, was bolstered by many great names that were then carved upon the temple’s walls; Aldwin, Chloe, Mathias, Johanna, Jannick, Katerina, Mirian, Tink, Hankok, Finn, Jiao, Elixir, Lumine, Haidar.

A Spear prayed only that Zeal would not rise and with him bring strife, add another ghost to hunt.

A Kihai implored for Wisdom.

A Myriad, for a path through the evils that had always singled their people out.

Another Myriad, for the passion and duty zeal spoke to.

A Caeli for a blade to beat back the darkness.

New Variman itself, for a hope most had not dared to hope.

It is difficult to say there was a single motivation for all beyond a weariness and desire for benevolent guidance, a change from the indifferent tricks of the demiurges before. Each member had their own musing of the kind of Archon they wished for, though none thought that a seventh would be possible.
